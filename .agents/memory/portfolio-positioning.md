The portfolio is for applying to Presales Engineer and Technical Lead roles within a business. It should communicate hiring relevance, customer-facing technical judgment, solution architecture, and leadership rather than invite freelance projects or frame the site as a project marketplace.

**Why:** The user clarified on 29 September 2026 that the site is intended for role applications, not finding projects.

**How to apply:** Keep hero copy, calls to action, project walkthrough descriptions, capabilities, and contact language oriented toward recruiters, hiring managers, enterprise customers, and prospective teams.
